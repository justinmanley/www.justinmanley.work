<div class="intertext">
I lean forward to get a better view of the painting, but the couple behind me is in the way.
</div>

<figure><img src="./images/vertical_counterpoint_in_red_with_self.jpg"></img></figure>

<div class="intertext">
When I stand in front of the painting, my own obscuring figure fills the frame.
</div>

<figure><img src="./images/vertical_counterpoint_in_red_with_bottom_of_picture_frame.jpg"></img></figure>

<div class="intertext">
Perhaps the artist, who throughout his work played incessantly with reflections, would be tickled that these pictures are so bedeviled.
</div>

<figure><img src="./images/vertical_counterpoint_in_red_with_pictures.jpg"></img></figure>

<div class="intertext">
Or does the painting, in fact, have the upper hand as it absorbs the gallery into its world of just-barely-perceptible variations of taupe?
</div>

<figure><img src="./images/vertical_counterpoint_in_red_with_ceiling_air_vents.jpg"></img></figure>

<div class="intertext">
If I just bend my knees and lean to the side a little further, perhaps I can shunt the air vent on the wall behind me up and out of the picture.
</div>

<figure><img src="./images/vertical_counterpoint_in_red_with_smudge.jpg"></img></figure>

<div class="intertext">

Sitting at the end of the bench in the middle of the room, I can see the painting at last. <br /> <br /> It's better in person, I promise.

</div>
