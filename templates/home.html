Welcome to my world

I'm making a photo book! Pairwise is a book of pairs of pictures along with short texts about the histories behind the images.

I’m bringing this project into the physical world: first as a limited-edition preview of 100 copies shipping in time for the holidays, and then as a full-length book in 2027.

Subscribe to follow along as I sort through images, visit archives, test paper samples, and watch freshly-printed pages pour out of the printer. Subscribers also get first word when the limited-edition booklet goes live.
